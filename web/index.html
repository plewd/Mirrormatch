    Basics Abilities How bots think Pitfalls Strategy tips

    Basics

    • Win by bringing the enemy to 0 HP (both start with ). After s, whoever has more HP wins; equal HP is a draw.
    • Controls: right-click (or hold) to walk; Q Bolt and W Dash toward the cursor; E uses your pickup; R plays again. Watching: Space pauses, ←/→ step one tick.
    • Casting roots you. Most abilities have a short cast time during which you can't move or dash. That window is when you're easiest to hit, and the main thing to play around.
    • Pickups appear one at a time, first at s, then every s (at most on the map, never two of the same). Each one spawns exactly as far from you as from the enemy, so it's a fair race. You hold one, single use. If both players touch one on the same tick, both get it.
    • The center heal appears at s and s after each time it's taken, for + HP. Its countdown ring is visible to both. You can take it at full HP just to deny it.

    Abilities

    Numbers come straight from the game's config, so they're always current.

    How bots think

    Rule words below are colored like their blocks in Build: move ability condition mode

    • Priority, not steps. On each decision the bot checks its whole list at once, against one snapshot of what it sees, and does the first matching move and the first matching ability. Lower rules are ignored for that decision.
    • How often, and how late: bots decide a few times a second and see the world slightly late, depending on difficulty: . That delay is what makes them beatable.
    • Moving and casting are separate channels. One decision can strafe and shoot.
    • Fall-through: if a matching action can't happen (Bolt on cooldown, dash while rooted, empty pickup slot), the next rule in that channel gets the turn. hold position and hold fire always "happen", so they stop the fall-through.
    • Modes switch immediately: rules below a switch to mode already see the new mode. Every bot starts in normal.
    • Chance is re-rolled every decision, so if 30% chance flickers. For a random plan that sticks, use chance to switch to mode, then act on if my mode is ….
    • Memory sensors let a bot react to what happened, not just what it sees now: for example seconds since enemy used dash or seconds since I got hit.

    Pitfalls

    • "Dodge above shoot" doesn't stop the shot. They're in different channels. Add hold fire if a shot is coming at me, above your shoot rule, or you'll start a cast (and get rooted) mid-dodge.
    • Committing too early: a cast started one decision before a shot appears can't be undone. Shoot when the enemy can't punish it: if enemy's cooldown on bolt > 0.3, or if enemy is casting.
    • Shields expire ( s). Against slow shots, raise them late: if seconds until a hit < 0.3.
    • Out of range shots are wasted cooldowns. Guard them with if in range: bolt.
    • Not sure what your bot is doing? Use Preview: the blocks firing right now glow, and the line under it says which rules won and how many fell through.

    Strategy tips

    • Close range wins trades. Up close, a Bolt arrives faster than anyone can react and step aside.
    • Bait the dash, then punish: a straight shot from mid-range makes a careful opponent dash. Their dash then has a s cooldown: rush in. (Load the baiter starter to see one way.)
    • Combos: Root, then Bolt the rooted target. Hook pulls them next to you and cancels their cast. Bolt them point-blank.
    • Walls block both sides' shots, so don't hide behind one you need to shoot through. Standing inside a wall doesn't protect you.
    • Own the center a second or two before the heal appears. The countdown is public.
